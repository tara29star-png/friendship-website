HOW SOME HELLO'S BECOMES THE BEGINNING OF SOMETHING BEAUTIFUL!
(PHASE : 1)
GRADE 5TH
IN a room of about 40 students, we never thought that in upcoming years we 
two, would have a such a strong bond! a bond  which existed before this lifetime, and gonna exist in upcoming
lifetimes too!!
well, things happened in our life's which made us closer.
and strangely where most of the relationships fades away when ;distances increases,
our friendship grew stronger with every increasig distance! rather from distances only
our bond grew stronger!

I came in Ambala in 5th grade mid, it was the time of half-yearlies 
examination.
Never knew that we would become such good friends.
In class 5th we didn't had much interaction,
only when all girls used to go out to  have lunch,
we used to talk little.
(PHASE : 2)
GRADE 6TH
I can't recall vividly but this was the time when we started 
sitting together i guess, we started eating lunch together,
I rember the moment when the lunch bell used to ring,
how fast we used to leave the class to go to the 
canteen, on the way,while coming down by stairs u used to always
say this: 

"jaldi ka kaam shaitan ka hota hai :)"

usually we used to buy mentos,(that all fruits flavour ones.)
Somewhere those vibrant colour and tasty flavours,
represent how our friendhip was becoming colourful too!
like how when u used to be absent somedays, my days used to feel
like i just have come to school with my body and my soul and heart isn't with me,
i guess it's true:

"WE don't miss the places,we miss those souls we used to meet there"

How our friendship was blooming, it was slow but beautiful process...
(PHASE: 3)
GRADE 7TH and 8TH
Class 7A was our class, RITU SABLOK MAM,
our class teacher, i guess she was the main reason
that our class, became the best one!
like each and every teacher who used to once come in 
our classroom used to say that this is the best class among 
all sections of class 7th!
We used to sit together,eat lunch together,
laugh together and we made so many memories,
moments, the ones we cherish the most!
somewhere , we started Trusting each other more,
we started sharing our happiness, sadness with each other,
like how when my dad got a promotion in his job, i 
shared the happiness with her in form of sweets!
and in return her mother sent us a box of samosas!
which i loved sooo, much! i remember the taste even now!heheee:)
those small gestures made our bond stronger!
also,
it was in these days of class 7th only,
in which we wrote our first poem.... 
(Never really knew back then, that we both gonna write many more poems in future!
our younger selves, must be in disbelief by knowing thiss!hehehe:))

Life was sooo much good those days!
(hmm... feeling nostalgic!:)
(PHASE: 4)
GRADE 9TH and COVID-19
This was the time when covid-19 pandemic started,
and everything changed, our schools got closed,
and we had to do online classes, and it ws new for all of us,
sitting in front of screens for hours, life was boring ,
in these days, we used to have little conversations after or 
sometimes before the classes but it used to mostly revolve around the 
studies,classes only. and sometimes we used to talk about how we
miss those school days when everything waas normal.
when the covid-19 cases decreased a bit, our school started offline classes
but it was odd-even roll number basis,sadly Divya never came to school,
in whole 9th grade offline classes period.
only it was during the exams she came, and honestly , i used to  miss
her alot,my face was always sad now without her presence,
in class.
(PHASE: 5)
THE DAY BEFORE I LEFT AMBALA!:(
11th February 2022, the day before i left Ambala,
huhh, sooo, much have changed since this day!, soo  much literally,
like within me as a person, and our friendship evolved too!
I remember this day very well, like it was just yesterday,
our packing was done, and on 12th we had to leave, at 5am in morning,
still somehow i convinced my parents to agree to let me go to
Abhyaas Academy and to Meet You at your home,
thanlfully they agreed,and before going to abhyaas academy,
i came to your home,  that is near INDIAN Roller Hotel right?(Check my memory:)) i guess so,
We talked for few hours, ate some things, laughed, and do you remember?
we even wrote a poetry too?


i'll add that soon here! :)



then it was the time to leave, i still remember the hug we shared,
i felt very sad, and teary eyed, but i tried to be strong,
as i had to attend my coaching classes.
leaving you that day was soo hard for me,
it was like i'm leaving behind an important part of my life,
my best friend, my lifer!
but i promised myself that no matter what happines,
mere distance cann never come in between our friendship,
and look here we are now at present time!
still connected soo strongly, soo deeply!


Written sooo, many poetries together, exploring many things ,
together,sharing our ideas and writings together,
growing together as individuals and as friends!
i feel soo blessed to have you in my life!
